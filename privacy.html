Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Data

Last updated: October 2026

The Mokuyoubi Team (Harutaka Shimizu) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following: account information such as email address and username provided during registration; gameplay data including match history and puzzle records; subscription status (for example, whether Premium is active); access logs and device information (such as IP address and OS version) for service improvement. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used to: provide, maintain, and improve the Service; manage subscriptions and Premium access; detect and prevent fraud; respond to inquiries; and send important service-related notices. We do not use your data for marketing purposes.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share or disclose it to third parties, except as required by law or as described in this policy. We may share limited information with service providers (such as cloud hosting) strictly as necessary for operations.

4. Payments and Subscription Providers

Web purchases of Hoshigo Premium are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited ("Paddle"), which acts as the Merchant of Record and reseller. Paddle collects and processes your payment and billing information (such as name, email address, billing country, and payment details) for the purpose of completing your order, handling taxes, fraud prevention, and refunds, under its own privacy notice: paddle.com/legal/privacy. We receive information about your order and subscription status from Paddle.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and Premium access across the web, iOS, and Android. RevenueCat receives your app user ID and purchase information for this purpose. Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

5. Data Storage & Security

User information is stored on servers with appropriate security measures. However, we cannot guarantee absolute security over the internet. Please manage your account carefully and notify us immediately of any suspicious activity.

6. Use of Cookies

We may use cookies to maintain login sessions and improve service quality. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly without them.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. If we become aware that personal information of a child under 13 has been collected, we will promptly delete it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your account information. For account deletion or data export requests, please email support@mokuyoubi.org or contact us via GitHub Issues.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. Significant changes will be communicated within the Service.
