Paddle.com Market Limited ("Paddle") is the Merchant of Record and authorised reseller for web purchases of Hoshigo Premium. Refunds are processed by Paddle, not by the Mokuyoubi Team.

2. Refund period

You can request a refund within 14 days of the purchase date, for any reason. This applies to both monthly and yearly subscriptions, including renewal charges.

Please request your refund from Paddle at paddle.net. You can find your order using the email address you used at checkout and the receipt Paddle sent you. If you have any trouble, email us at support@mokuyoubi.org and we will help.

4. Processing time

Once a refund is approved, it may take several business days to appear, depending on your payment method and bank. When a refund is issued, Premium access for that purchase ends.

5. Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops future renewals. To get a refund for a charge, please request it within the 14-day period above.
